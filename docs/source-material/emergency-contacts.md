# Facility Emergency Contacts and Escalation SOP

## Emergency Operations Center
For active fires, severe water pipe bursts, smoke, or live electrical hazards, call the Facility Emergency Hotline immediately at +65 6789 0001 (internal speed-dial 9999).

## Fire and Smoke Emergency
If you see fire or smoke, first activate the nearest red fire alarm call point, then call the Facility Emergency Hotline at +65 6789 0001 (speed-dial 9999) to report the exact floor and location. Evacuate immediately using the marked stairwells; never use lifts during a fire. Close doors behind you as you leave to slow the spread of smoke, and proceed to the designated assembly point at the Ground Floor Forecourt. Do not attempt to fight a large or spreading fire yourself; a portable fire extinguisher should only be used on a small, contained fire and only if you have a safe, unobstructed escape route behind you. Wait at the assembly point for a headcount and do not re-enter the building until the Fire Safety Manager or Singapore Civil Defence Force (SCDF) gives the all-clear.

## Medical Emergency
For a medical emergency, collapse, severe injury, or any life-threatening condition, call the Facility Emergency Hotline at +65 6789 0001 (speed-dial 9999) and, for a life-threatening case, also dial 995 for an ambulance. State the patient's location, condition, and whether they are conscious and breathing. Send someone to the nearest lift lobby or entrance to guide paramedics to the casualty. Do not move an injured person unless they are in immediate danger, such as from fire or falling debris. Automated External Defibrillators (AEDs) are located at every lift lobby; only trained personnel or those guided by the 995 dispatcher should use one.

## Gas Leak or Suspected Gas Smell
If you smell gas, do not switch any electrical switches, lights, or equipment on or off, and do not use naked flames or mobile phones in the immediate area. Evacuate the affected area calmly, leaving doors open behind you to ventilate if it is safe to do so, and move to the open air well away from the building. Call the Facility Emergency Hotline at +65 6789 0001 (speed-dial 9999) from a safe distance to report the location and severity of the smell.

## Chemical Spill or Hazardous Material Exposure
For a chemical spill, hazardous material leak, or exposure incident, evacuate the immediate area and prevent others from entering or walking through the spill. Do not attempt to clean up an unknown substance yourself. Call the Facility Emergency Hotline at +65 6789 0001 (speed-dial 9999) and report the substance (if known), the affected location, and whether anyone has been exposed. If a person has chemical contact with skin or eyes, flush the affected area with water at the nearest eyewash station or tap for at least 15 minutes while help is on the way.

## Flooding and Water Damage
For major flooding, burst pipes, or water pouring from ceilings, walls, or floors, move away from the water and any nearby electrical outlets, switches, or equipment. Call the Facility Emergency Hotline at +65 6789 0001 (speed-dial 9999) to report the location so the water supply can be isolated. Do not attempt to shut off main water valves yourself unless you have been trained to do so. If water is near electrical panels or lifts, warn others in the area and avoid contact until Facilities confirms the power has been isolated.

## Power Outage
If power is lost, stay calm and remain where you are if it is safe; emergency lighting and exit signs are on backup power and will illuminate escape routes. Do not use lifts during a power outage. Report the outage and your location to the Facility Emergency Hotline at +65 6789 0001 (speed-dial 9999) or the 24/7 Security Control Room at +65 6789 0000. If you are trapped in a lift when power fails, follow the Lift Breakdown and Entrapment procedure below.

## Lift Breakdown and Entrapment
If trapped inside an elevator or observing a stalled lift car, press the yellow emergency alarm button inside the lift cabin for 3 seconds to establish direct two-way audio with the 24/7 Lift Operations Center (+65 6789 0002). Do not attempt to force doors open.

## Structural Damage or Falling Debris
If you notice cracks, sagging ceilings, falling debris, or any sign of structural damage, move away from the affected area immediately and warn others nearby not to enter. Do not attempt to inspect or touch the damaged structure. Call the Facility Emergency Hotline at +65 6789 0001 (speed-dial 9999) to report the exact location so the area can be cordoned off and inspected by a structural engineer.

## Earthquake or Sudden Building Shaking
If the building shakes suddenly, drop, cover, and hold on under a sturdy desk or table away from windows and heavy furniture that could fall. Stay indoors until the shaking stops. Once it stops, evacuate via the stairwells if instructed to do so by Security or the fire alarm, and proceed to the assembly point at the Ground Floor Forecourt. Do not use lifts. Report any injuries or visible structural damage to the Facility Emergency Hotline at +65 6789 0001 (speed-dial 9999).

## Severe Weather and Storm Warnings
During severe weather such as lightning, heavy storms, or flash flood warnings, avoid outdoor areas, rooftop terraces, and covered walkways with exposed metal railings. Move away from windows and glass facades. Basement carpark users should be alert to flash flood advisories and follow any Security instructions to relocate vehicles. For weather-related hazards on the premises, such as water ingress or a damaged awning, contact the Facility Emergency Hotline at +65 6789 0001 (speed-dial 9999).

## Active Threat, Intruder, or Violent Incident
If you encounter an armed intruder, active violence, or an immediate threat to personal safety, prioritize your own safety first: run and evacuate if there is a safe path out, hide and lock or barricade the door if evacuation is not safe, and only fight back as an absolute last resort. Silence your phone and call the 24/7 Security Control Room at +65 6789 0000 as soon as it is safe to do so, or dial 999 for Police if the threat is immediate and life-threatening. Do not activate the fire alarm unless there is also a fire, as this may direct people into the path of danger. When Police or Security arrive, keep your hands visible and follow their instructions.

## Bomb Threat or Suspicious Item
If you receive a bomb threat by phone, keep the caller talking if possible, note the exact wording, any background noises, and the caller's voice, and do not hang up if avoidable; alert a colleague to call the 24/7 Security Control Room at +65 6789 0000 on another line without alarming the caller. If you discover a suspicious unattended item, do not touch, move, or open it. Evacuate the immediate area, keep others away, and report the item's exact location to Security immediately at +65 6789 0000. Do not use mobile phones or two-way radios within 10 meters of a suspicious item, as transmissions may pose a risk.

## Security Control Room
The 24/7 Security Control Room is located on Basement 1 Room B1-04, phone +65 6789 0000. Use this line for non-life-threatening security concerns, lost persons, suspicious behaviour, unattended items, or to request an escort after hours.

## General Emergency Escalation Checklist
For any emergency: 1) ensure your own immediate safety first, 2) call the Facility Emergency Hotline at +65 6789 0001 (speed-dial 9999) or, for life-threatening situations, dial 995 (medical), 995 (fire and rescue via SCDF), or 999 (Police) directly, 3) clearly state the nature of the emergency, the exact location (building, floor, zone or room code), and whether anyone is injured, 4) do not hang up until told to do so by the operator, and 5) follow all instructions from Security, Facilities staff, or emergency responders, including evacuation routes and assembly points. When in doubt about whether a situation is serious enough to report, report it anyway.
