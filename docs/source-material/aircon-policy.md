# HVAC and Air-Conditioning Temperature Policy

## Standard Cooling Setpoints
Central air-conditioning is scheduled from 07:30 to 19:00 Monday through Friday. Standard temperature setpoints are maintained between 23.0°C and 25.0°C across all office floors in compliance with the National Energy Efficiency Standard.

## After-Hours Aircon Extension
Occupants requiring air-conditioning extension beyond 19:00 or during weekends must submit an HVAC extension request via the facilities portal at least 4 hours prior to the requested cooling window.

## Temperature Dispute and Hot/Cold Complaints
If room temperature deviates beyond 21.0°C or 26.5°C for more than 30 minutes, submit a temperature complaint with the zone or room code. A facilities technician will be dispatched to verify damper calibration.
