# Building Operating Hours and Access Rules

## General Building Hours
The building main lobby and standard facility areas are open Monday through Friday from 07:00 to 22:00. On Saturdays, the building is open from 08:00 to 18:00. The building is closed to general visitors on Sundays and Public Holidays.

## After-Hours Access
Authorized occupants and staff with valid keycard credentials may access the building 24/7 via the North Turnstiles. Visitors requiring after-hours entry must be registered with Security at least 24 hours in advance.

## Facility Desk Operating Hours
The Facilities Management Helpdesk on Level 1 operates from 08:30 to 17:30 Monday through Friday. For urgent facility issues outside helpdesk hours, contact the 24/7 Security Duty Office.
