# Building Facility Directory and Asset Locations

## Overview
This document serves as the authoritative facility directory and equipment location register for the campus building. Automated systems and maintenance personnel reference this directory to resolve equipment designations to their physical locations.

## Vertical Transportation (Lifts and Elevators)

### Passenger Lifts
- **Passenger Lift 1**: Main Tower, Lift Lobby A (Serves Basement 2 through Level 12).
- **Passenger Lift 2**: Main Tower, Lift Lobby A (Serves Basement 2 through Level 12).
- **Passenger Lift 3**: West Wing, Lift Lobby B on Level 1 (Serves Basement 1 through Level 8). Dedicated passenger car with digital floor indicator and access badge reader.
- **Passenger Lift 4**: West Wing, Lift Lobby B on Level 1 (Serves Basement 1 through Level 8).
- **Passenger Lift 5**: East Wing, Executive Core (Serves Ground Level through Level 5).

### Service and Special Lifts
- **Service / Cargo Lift 1**: Loading Bay & Service Core (Serves Basement 2 through Level 12). Heavy cargo transport.
- **Carpark Shuttle Lift**: Carpark Basement 1 & 2 to Ground Level Main Lobby.
- **Accessible Platform Lift**: Level 1 Auditorium Entrance (Wheelchair accessibility).

## HVAC and Mechanical Equipment
- **Central Chillers 1 and 2**: Basement 2 Central Plant Room.
- **Cooling Towers 1, 2, and 3**: Rooftop Mechanical Deck.
- **Air Handling Units (AHU)**: Located in dedicated AHU utility rooms on each floor (Rooms 109, 209, 309, 409, 509, 609, 709, 809).
- **Primary Water Pumps**: Basement 2 Pump Room B2-08.

## Meeting and Educational Spaces
- **Meeting Rooms 101–105**: Level 1 Administrative Wing.
- **Meeting Rooms 201–208**: Level 2 Collaboration Zone (Meeting Room 204 is opposite Breakout Area 2A).
- **Meeting Rooms 301–308**: Level 3 Research Wing.
- **Seminar Rooms 1–4**: Ground Level / Level 1 East Concourse.
- **Seminar Rooms 5–8**: Level 2 Innovation Block.
- **Auditorium**: Level 1 Central Atrium (Capacity 350).

## Operational Support and Security Desks
- **Facilities Management Helpdesk**: Level 1 Main Foyer (Room 101, ext. 8101).
- **24/7 Security Duty Office**: Basement 1 (Room B1-04, phone +65 6789 0000).
- **Fire Command Centre (FCC)**: Ground Level adjacent to Main Foyer Security Checkpoint.
